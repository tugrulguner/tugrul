02 About me

Curiosity took me from materials science to the systems that make AI work in production.

I began coding and working with machine learning during my master’s degree in 2012.

I learned the traditional approaches, competed on Kaggle, and spent years engineering features by hand. Deep learning opened a different direction. I used computer vision with microscope images during my first postdoc, then worked on image reconstruction during my second.

As the models became more capable, I became increasingly interested in everything required to make them useful beyond an experiment: the backend, deployment, infrastructure, and operating practices that keep AI reliable at scale. That pulled me into MLOps and production engineering. The work fits how my mind operates: solve difficult systems problems, structure decisions around evidence, and build things that must hold up in real use.

ModePot is where I build tools and systems I want to exist. Passionately Curious is where I make difficult scientific ideas accessible. My personal essays examine the ambition, mortality, curiosity, and future behind the work.

My ambition as a manager

Build high-performing teams where talented people take ownership and raise the standard for one another.

I give people the context and authority to make good decisions, with explicit ownership and standards that make autonomy work. I operate well under pressure, especially when timelines are tight, priorities are shifting, and difficult decisions cannot wait. I bring structure to ambiguity, make the necessary tradeoffs, and keep the team moving without lowering engineering standards. As I take on greater organizational scope, I want to stay close enough to the technology to make sound decisions while helping people grow into harder problems, challenge ideas with evidence, and ship work that matters.

04 Technical range

Agentic engineering, from harnesses to production systems.

I design and operate agentic workflows across Hermes Agent, Claude Code, and Cursor’s agent capabilities, including CLI-based execution. I configure task-appropriate model routing, coordinate multiple agents across distinct tasks, and delegate focused work to subagents. I combine project-specific context, reusable skills, and tool integrations with explicit execution boundaries and verification through tests, review, and execution evidence.

My experience spans agent orchestration, reusable skills, evaluation, low-level LLM optimization, backend architecture, and production infrastructure. I work across the harness, application, and infrastructure layers rather than treating the agent as a standalone chat interface.
