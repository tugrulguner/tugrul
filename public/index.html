01 The story

Curiosity took me from materials science to production AI. Building kept me moving.

I love the point where a difficult idea stops being abstract: the architecture becomes clear, the team finds its rhythm, and something useful begins to exist.

I started as a materials scientist, earned a Ph.D., completed two postdoctoral fellowships, and published research before following the same curiosity into machine learning and infrastructure. Today I lead two cross-functional engineering pods at CDAI, while still prototyping, changing architectures, and turning ideas into production systems.

I want to understand reality without comforting shortcuts, then help build a future I can experience rather than merely leave behind. That instinct became ModePot, my open-source playground for simpler software; Passionately Curious, where I explore the science I cannot stop thinking about; and personal essays about why I keep questioning and building.
