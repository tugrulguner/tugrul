01 The story

AI becomes useful when its freedom has a shape.

The most interesting engineering problem is not making a model say more. It is deciding exactly where software should be deterministic, where an agent may decide, and how people stay in control.

I lead AI engineering at CDAI and build open-source tools in the gaps I keep encountering: interfaces that should translate cleanly, APIs that need bounded judgment, runtimes that should adapt without complicating application code, and simulations where change must remain replayable.

That work became ModePot—a family of projects built around one idea: powerful systems become easier to trust when their contracts, authority, and execution are visible.
