01 The story

From materials science to production AI systems.

My work crosses scientific research, machine learning infrastructure, product strategy, and engineering leadership. The common thread is turning uncertain problems into systems with explicit behavior and measurable outcomes.

I earned a Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering before moving into machine learning and production infrastructure. I now lead AI engineering at CDAI, where I shape the technical roadmap and lead a cross-functional team building systems used by thousands of people.

I also created ModePot, an open-source family built around simpler APIs, modern runtimes, performance, and clear boundaries between deterministic software and agent-owned decisions.
