02 About me

Curiosity took me from materials science to production AI. Leadership expanded what I could build.

I am at my best setting direction and building strong teams while staying close enough to the technical work to shape architecture and ship production systems.

I started as a materials scientist, earned a Ph.D., completed two postdoctoral fellowships, and published research before following the same curiosity into machine learning and infrastructure. That path taught me to move between first principles and execution, then help other people do their best work around a shared direction.

I believe executive responsibility does not require distance from engineering. My preferred leadership model combines broader organizational scope with hands-on involvement when architecture, prototypes, or production systems need direct judgment. Outside work, that instinct became ModePot, Passionately Curious, and personal essays about why I keep questioning and building.
